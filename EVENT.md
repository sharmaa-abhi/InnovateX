## About the Event

### Detailed Description

Notion Unlocked is an exclusive community gathering designed for people looking to improve the way they work, organize information, and collaborate. The event explores how Notion can support efficient workflows, project management, knowledge organization, and team collaboration.

Participants can also explore Notion AI and Notion Agents through live demonstrations and practical real-world use cases.

### Main Objectives

- Improve professional workflows and productivity with Notion.
- Organize information and knowledge more effectively.
- Manage projects and team collaboration in a unified workspace.
- Demonstrate practical uses of Notion AI and Notion Agents.
- Provide direct opportunities to ask questions and discuss applications.
- Help attendees exchange ideas and build meaningful professional connections.

### What Participants Will Learn

- An overview of the Notion ecosystem and its core features.
- Ways professionals and teams can organize information and manage projects.
- Practical workspace, documentation, knowledge-management, and collaboration workflows.
- Intelligent and AI-powered productivity capabilities through Notion AI and Agents.
- Practical applications discussed during the interactive Q&A.

### Key Highlights

- Introduction to Notion
- Live demonstration: Notion in Action
- Live demonstration: Notion AI & Agents
- Interactive Q&A with the Notion team
- Professional networking with founders, operators, product managers, builders, and other attendees
- Refreshments and networking
